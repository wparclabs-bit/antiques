# 03: Comparables & Valuation

**What to build:** Evidence-based pricing for every Item: Comparables (source marketplace, link, date, sold price with currency, notes) recorded against an Item, one-click sold-listing search links for eBay and Etsy generated from the Item's attributes, and an Owner-set Valuation price range with full history kept over time.

**Blocked by:** 02 (Items must exist to hold Comparables).

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Comparables can be recorded against an Item: source marketplace, URL, date, sold price with currency, and notes
- [ ] Sold-listing search links for eBay and Etsy are generated from the Item's attributes in one click
- [ ] The Owner can set a Valuation price range on an Item; setting Valuations is an Owner-only action
- [ ] Valuation history is retained per Item and viewable over time
- [ ] Seam tests cover Comparables recording, deterministic search-link generation, the Owner-only Valuation action, and history retention
