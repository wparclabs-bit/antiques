# Antique Furniture Marketplaces — Europe/Netherlands focus (for selling from Indonesia)

**Research date:** 2026-10-24. Commission rates and policies change; every number below is linked. Items marked ⚠ could not be confirmed from the platform's own pages and need verification before the app hard-codes them.

**Method note:** Catawiki, Vinterior, Selency and Incollect block automated page fetches (HTTP 403), so some figures come from search-engine snapshots of those pages, quoted as such. Where possible the platform's own help/terms pages were read.

## Catawiki (Amsterdam) — the key NL-based option

**What it is:** an online curated auction house, HQ in Amsterdam. Sellers submit an object with photos for free valuation; one of ~200 in-house auction experts assesses it and, if accepted, schedules it into a themed weekly auction where buyers bid ([Catawiki help: how selling works](https://www.catawiki.com/en/help/become-a-seller/how-does-selling-on-catawiki-work)). It is *not* an open classifieds site and *not* a fixed-price marketplace — every sale goes through an expert-vetted auction with an estimated price range and usually a reserve.

**Fees:** "Seller success fee" of **12.5% of the selling price plus an additional fixed amount**, charged only when the object sells ("no sale, no fee") ([help page](https://www.catawiki.com/en/help/seller-success-fee-vat/what-is-the-seller-success-fee), [register page](https://www.catawiki.com/seller/register)). The fixed amount is tiered — reported from €0.75 (sales under €75) up to €10 (sales over €1,000), excluding VAT ([Estimonia directory summary](https://estimonia.com/directory/catawiki), matching the [seller terms PDF](https://cdn.catawiki.net/assets/marketing/terms/2026/web/seller-terms/seller-terms-en-092026.pdf) formula "12.5% ... with an additional fixed amount"). Buyers separately pay a ~9% buyer protection fee plus a payment fee, so hammer price ≠ buyer's total ([ReefAPI notes](https://reefapi.com/catawiki-api) ⚠ third-party).

**Seller acceptance & geography:** "Anyone can submit and sell at Catawiki" — both private and professional sellers, with an expert gate per object ([help centre](https://www.catawiki.com/en/help/become-a-seller/how-does-selling-on-catawiki-work)). Non-EU sellers do operate on the platform (e.g. guides for Ukraine/India-based sellers shipping into the EU with tracked couriers) ([GlobalPost guide](https://globalpost.ua/en/selling-antiques-on-catawiki-how-to-successfully-enter-the-eu-market) ⚠ third-party). Sellers must set shipping rates "for every region where Catawiki has bidders", and handle customs when shipping outside their own customs zone ([shipping-cost guide](https://www.catawiki.com/en/stories/5249-everything-you-need-to-know-about-setting-shipping-costs-on-catawiki), [customs help](https://www.catawiki.com/en/help/seller-customs-and-taxes/do-all-sellers-need-to-deal-with-customs)). ⚠ Not confirmed: whether payout rails work for an Indonesian bank account, and whether large furniture is accepted via Catawiki's own "smart shipping" network or seller-arranged freight (smart shipping covers EU and beyond for professional sellers, more limited for private sellers — [help](https://www.catawiki.com/en/help/smart-shipping/which-countries-are-covered-by-smart-shipping)).

**Furniture fit:** good — Catawiki runs recurring Design/Furniture auctions and has a dedicated [selling channel for special objects](https://www.catawiki.com/sell). Single lots rather than shops, which suits a family selling inherited pieces one by one.

## Marktplaats (NL classifieds)

**What it is:** the largest Dutch classifieds/trading platform — 8 million visitors/month, 350,000 new listings/day; founded 1999, bought by eBay in 2004 ($290M), sold with eBay Classifieds Group to Adevinta in 2020/21 ([Adevinta brand page](https://adevinta.com/brand/marktplaats), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marktplaats.nl)).

**Viability from Indonesia: effectively no.** Three compounding blockers: (1) the site and its buyer culture are Dutch-language and domestic — the default expectation is local pickup ("ophalen"), not international freight; (2) payments on shipping transactions run through Marktplaats' payment system with payouts via Online Payment Platform to a **linked bank account** with identity verification, and buyer payments reference SEPA ([OPP Marktplaats payout support](https://onlinepaymentplatform.com/nl/support/marktplaats/uitbetaling-nog-niet-ontvangen), [Marktplaats SEPA help](https://help.marktplaats.nl/articles/nl_NL/Knowledge/betalen-met-sepa-via-marktplaats)) — an Indonesian bank account is not a SEPA account, so payouts are a practical blocker ⚠ (inferred from payout docs; Marktplaats does not publish an explicit "must live in NL" rule); (3) Marktplaats' own safety guidance warns users about foreign parties, so a foreign seller profile faces trust friction ([safe-selling help](https://help.marktplaats.nl/s/article/tips-voor-veilig-verkopen-op-marktplaats)). **The realistic route is a Netherlands-based relative or friend who lists on the family's behalf** — worth supporting in the app as an "agent sells locally" scenario, but not as a direct listing channel.

## 1stDibs

**What it is:** US-based luxury marketplace for antiques, design and furniture, with 4,700+ sellers globally and an expanded European presence since 2022 ([investor news](https://investors.1stdibs.com/news/news-details/2022/Leading-Online-Luxury-Marketplace-1stDibs-to-Expand-European-Presence/default.aspx)).

**Seller requirements:** application + approval; at least two trade references verifying "a business in good standing" with top-quality inventory ([support: Applying to Sell](https://support.1stdibs.com/hc/en-us/articles/14490330236955-Applying-to-Sell-on-1stDibs), [application form](https://www.1stdibs.com/dealer/application-form)). It is built for professional dealers, galleries and makers — not private families.

**Fees:** tiered monthly subscription + per-sale commission; the tier with lower monthly fee has higher commission ([support: monthly fee](https://support.1stdibs.com/hc/en-us/articles/17416136155291-What-Is-the-Monthly-Fee-for-1stDibs), [support: commission](https://support.1stdibs.com/hc/en-us/articles/17416185631387-What-Percentage-Does-1stDibs-Take-from-a-Sale)). Exact current numbers are not published on the public pages; third-party reports put commissions at ~15–25% of sale price ([StableCommerce 2026 fee breakdown](https://stablecommerce.ai/blog/1stdibs-fees-2026) ⚠ unverified).

**Furniture fit:** excellent for museum-grade European design, but the vetting + subscription + dealer references make it unrealistic for an inherited-closet seller. **No public sold prices** — sold listings' final prices are not displayed, a long-standing collector complaint; 1stDibs only runs an internal AI price-range tool ([Business of Home, Nov 2025](https://businessofhome.com/articles/1stdibs-revises-controversial-pricing-tool)). Useful as an *asking-price* reference only.

## Vinterior (UK)

**What it is:** London-based marketplace focused on vintage and antique furniture; ~1,800–2,000 sellers across Europe (reported "30+ countries"), 200,000–400,000 live one-of-a-kind products ([We Work Remotely company profile](https://weworkremotely.com/company/vinterior), [Guardian buying guide](https://www.theguardian.com/lifeandstyle/2023/apr/15/ignore-trends-ask-for-more-photos-and-haggle-how-to-buy-vintage-like-a-pro)).

**Fees:** "No monthly fees, listing fees, or hidden fees — only pay when you sell. Commission: 17% on the first £2,500 per item, then 10% on any amount above £2,500" ([Vinterior sell page](https://www.vinterior.co/pages/how-to-sell) — figures retrieved via search snapshot; the page 403-blocks bots; the site's [terms](https://www.vinterior.co/pages/terms_of_service) also reference a 20% figure on purchases, so verify at signup ⚠).

**Seller requirements/geography:** sellers are onboarded across Europe; Vinterior runs an [EU Collection](https://www.vinterior.co/showcases/eu-collection) with logistics partner GoVintage delivering EU-sourced pieces to UK buyers. Listings are reviewed by the curation team before going live ([House Beautiful on Vinterior Resale](https://www.housebeautiful.com/uk/lifestyle/shopping/a44849422/vinterior-new-resale-platform)). ⚠ Unknown: whether a seller based in Indonesia (outside Europe) can register, and how payouts work for non-UK sellers (payout provider not documented publicly — Stripe is rumoured but unconfirmed). Treat as an EU-presence channel for now.

**Furniture fit:** the closest category match of any platform here — the entire site *is* vintage furniture.

## Selency (France)

**What it is:** Paris-founded curated marketplace for vintage furniture, antiques and decor ("the first online flea market"), operating country sites for France, UK, Belgium, Netherlands, with "custom delivery in Europe" and expert-moderated listings ([Selency UK how-to-sell](https://www.selency.co.uk/page/how-to-sell), [Fleamapket profile](https://www.fleamapket.com/listing/selency)).

**Fees:** commission only on sold items — **~15% for professional sellers** ([Ronati marketplace guide PDF](https://www.coastalcommunities.co.uk/content_uploads/_wp-content_uploads_2022_03_Ronati-Marketplace-Guide-2020-38.pdf) ⚠ third-party, 2020-era; Selency's own pages 403-block fetches). Selency's [terms PDF](https://share.selency.com/CGV/cgv.pdf) defines "professional sellers" as those with €15,000 turnover or 80 orders in 12 months — i.e. there are also private-seller tracks with different terms.

**Seller requirements/geography:** no public page states country eligibility for sellers; the delivery network and payout rails are European. ⚠ Unknown whether a non-EU seller can register; professional registration asks for a Business Tax Id (from the signup flow). Treat like Vinterior: EU-presence channel, else excluded.

**Furniture fit:** strong (furniture is its headline category), but it is an *asking-price* marketplace — no public sold-price archive; sold items just get a "vendu" label and drop out of search.

## Incollect

**What it is:** New York-based curated marketplace for antiques, fine art and mid-century, positioned as dealer/gallery storefronts with an interior-designer trade program ([incollect.com](https://www.incollect.com), [IN THE TRADE program](https://www.incollect.com/trade)).

**Fees:** zero commission for sellers and buyers; revenue via flat monthly subscription instead — launch-era tiers $295/$395/$495 (up to $695) per month by listing count ([Maison Gerard press release via search](https://www.incollect.com) ⚠ current pricing unpublished; contact required).

**Seller requirements:** vetted professional dealers. **Furniture fit:** top-end European antiques do appear, but the subscription + vetting exclude a private Indonesian family. Value for this project is as a *reference-price* source for high-end pieces, not a channel.

## Other platforms that surfaced

- **Pamono** (Berlin-born design marketplace): acquired by **Chairish** (US) in Aug 2021 ([Surface Mag](https://www.surfacemag.com/articles/chairish-acquires-pamono)); works exclusively with professional dealers; commissions follow Chairish's model (reported ~20% vintage tiers ⚠ third-party). Effectively a US-governed channel now.
- **LoveAntiques.com** (UK): ~43,600 antiques from 250+ verified dealers, listings vetted by the team, dealers arrange worldwide shipping ([loveantiques.com](https://www.loveantiques.com)). Dealer-only — not for private sellers.
- **Proantic** (FR): self-described first European dealer website; free listings, no commission for professional dealers ([proantic.com](https://www.proantic.com)), but weak seller reviews (TrustScore ~2/5 ⚠ Trustpilot via search).
- **Trocadero** (US, since 1998): dealer storefronts, commission-free hosting, "American, European and Asian dealers" ([trocadero.com](http://www.trocadero.com/index.html)). Dated directory model; dealer-only.
- **Auction aggregators — The Saleroom, Invaluable, LiveAuctioneers:** online bidding portals for physical auction houses; see comps section below.

## Selling antique furniture from Indonesia — constraints

1. **Indonesian export law is the hard gate.** Law No. 11/2010 on Cultural Conservation defines *cagar budaya* as objects typically **≥50 years old** that carry special historical/scientific/cultural value, and such objects may leave Indonesia only with government permission (framed around research/special interest) ([UU 11/2010 text](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/27798/UU%20Nomor%2011%20Tahun%202010.pdf)). Trade Ministry Reg. 45/2019 lists cultural-heritage goods under HS heading **97.05** (collections and collectors' pieces of historical/antique interest) as export-prohibited ([Permendag 45/2019](https://jdih.kemendag.go.id/uploads/peraturan/2019/1785664419_f7b9e6053f30d7ba.pdf)); customs actively intercepts undeclared cultural goods ([Bea Cukai example](https://www.facebook.com/beacukairi/posts/1306410444866812)). Assessment is not purely age-based, so mid-century teak furniture may be exportable while a colonial-era piece may not ⚠ — the app should push users to verify with the Ministry of Culture/customs before any listing ships.
2. **Freight is a volume business.** Furniture ships as LCL sea freight: Jakarta→Rotterdam runs roughly **$125–155 per CBM** (≈$630–780 for a 5 CBM / 750 kg shipment) with 40–48 day transit; all-in cost after CFS, port and clearance fees is typically **$140–200/CBM**; a typical 4–6 piece furniture order is 6–12 CBM; above ~13–15 CBM a 20ft container beats LCL ([FreightAmigo lane page](https://www.freightamigo.com/en/routes/shipping-from-indonesia-to-netherlands), [Suaid Global note](https://www.freightamigo.com/en/routes/shipping-from-indonesia-to-netherlands) ⚠ aggregator quotes — get forwarder quotes). Add export crating costs.
3. **EU import side is mild for antiques.** Original artworks, antiques and collectors' items enter the EU **duty-free** ([Convelio VAT guide](https://www.convelio.com/en/blog/blogpost/convelio-art-transport-vat-guide)); the Netherlands applies its **reduced 9% VAT** rate at import for qualifying art/antiques, with the art.23 *invoerregeling* available to defer the cash flow ([Belastingdienst: kunst/antiek tariff](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/tarieven_en_vrijstellingen/goederen_9_btw/kunst_verzamelvoorwerpen_antiek/kunst_verzamelvoorwerpen_en_antiek), [Belastingdienst: invoerregeling](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/btw-goederen-importeren-leveren)). Note the EU marketplace "deemed supplier" VAT relief covers imports up to €150 only — furniture consignments will exceed that, so non-EU sellers selling B2C into the EU face real import-VAT formalities ⚠ needs a tax adviser ([KVK overview on selling via foreign marketplaces](https://www.kvk.nl/internationaal/verkopen-via-buitenlandse-marktplaatsen)).
4. **Platform-level non-EU friction:** Catawiki is the main marketplace that explicitly welcomes submissions from anyone and documents non-EU sellers shipping into the EU ([help centre](https://www.catawiki.com/en/help/become-a-seller/how-does-selling-on-catawiki-work)). Vinterior/Selency payout geography is undocumented (assume EU-presence needed ⚠). Marktplaats requires a linked bank account via Online Payment Platform ([OPP support](https://onlinepaymentplatform.com/nl/support/marktplaats/uitbetaling-nog-niet-ontvangen)) — practical blocker without a SEPA account. 1stDibs/Incollect require dealer vetting and (Incollect) a monthly subscription.

## Practical implications for the app

- **Add Catawiki as the flagship European Listing channel** for furniture: Amsterdam-based, open to any seller with an expert gate, 12.5% + small fixed fee, and the app's strength (photo/description/provenance generation) maps directly onto Catawiki's valuation-submission workflow. Verify Indonesian payout support before shipping the feature.
- **Use Catawiki closed lots + LiveAuctioneers results (+ The Saleroom) as the Comparables workflow for furniture**, layering them over the existing eBay sold-listings source; treat 1stDibs/Vinterior/Selency/Incollect as asking-price context, clearly labeled "asking, not realized" in the UI.
- **Exclude Marktplaats, 1stDibs, Incollect, LoveAntiques, Proantic, Trocadero as direct channels** for this family (local-pickup culture + SEPA payout, dealer vetting/references, monthly subscriptions, dealer-only models) — but support a "Netherlands-based relative sells on my behalf" agent mode for Marktplaats if the family has one.
- **Gate Vinterior and Selency behind an "EU presence required" flag**: both are category-perfect and fee-reasonable (17%/10% and ~15%), but neither documents non-EU seller onboarding; revisit if the family establishes an EU intermediary.
- **Build an export-compliance step into the listing wizard**: age/provenance questionnaire against UU 11/2010 + HS 97.05, with a "verify with Ministry of Culture/customs before shipping" checkpoint — this is a legal gate, not a formality.
- **Include a freight cost estimator in every furniture listing draft**: CBM from dimensions → LCL Jakarta–Rotterdam ≈ $140–200/CBM all-in + crating, plus a 40–48 day delivery expectation, so listings quote realistic shipping to EU buyers (and the family can decide between premium door-to-door vs. buyer-arranges-freight).
