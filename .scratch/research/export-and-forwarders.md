## 1. Indonesian Export Regulations for Antiques

### Legal definition / age threshold
The controlling law is **UU No. 11 Tahun 2010 tentang Cagar Budaya** (Law 11/2010 on Cultural Conservation). A "Benda Cagar Budaya" (movable cultural property) is defined in Pasal 1 as natural/man-made objects connected with Indonesian culture and history ([Wikisource full text](https://id.wikisource.org/wiki/Undang-Undang_Republik_Indonesia_Nomor_11_Tahun_2010); [official PDF, BPHN](https://bphn.go.id/data/documents/10uu011.pdf)). Pasal 5 sets the practical threshold: an object becomes eligible for cagar budaya status if it is **at least 50 years old** (or represents a distinctive style period of at least 50 years) and meets significance criteria (historical, scientific, educational, religious, and/or cultural value). The Ministry of Trade's export ban list uses the same criteria ([PakGiman quoting Permendag 45/2019 — secondary source](https://pakgiman.com/barang-dilarang-ekspor)).

**Practical meaning:** an item does NOT have to be officially registered to be caught by the rules — anything plausibly 50+ years old can be treated as a suspected cultural property ("Objek Diduga Cagar Budaya", ODCB) until cleared.

### BPCB assessment and the key certificate
Provincial **Balai Pelestarian Cagar Budaya (BPCB)** offices assess suspected cultural property. Since late 2024/2025 they have been renamed **Balai Pelestarian Kebudayaan (BPK)** and moved to the new **Kementerian Kebudayaan** under Perpres No. 190/2024 (e.g., [Balai Pelestarian Kebudayaan Bali](https://balaibudayabali.com); [Permenkebud 2/2026](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/410541/permenkebud-no-2-tahun-2026.pdf)). Under **Permendikbudristek No. 36/2023** (Register Nasional Cagar Budaya), an owner registers an ODCB, experts study it, and a **"Surat Keterangan Bukan Cagar Budaya" (SKBCB)** — a certificate that the item is NOT a cultural property — is issued within 3 working days of the study ([Permendikbudristek 36/2023 PDF](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/321081/permendikbudristek-no-36-tahun-2023.pdf)). For antiques that are *not* registered cagar budaya, the SKBCB is the critical document customs expects at export; art-export guides also list a detailed commercial invoice, certificate of authenticity, CITES permit for protected materials (ivory, crocodile skin, etc.), and ISPM-15 fumigation for wooden crates ([HSH Logistik guide](https://hsh.co.id/apa-saja-dokumen-yang-dibutuhkan-untuk-ekspor-barang-seni)).

### Export permit process (items that ARE cagar budaya)
- Pasal 68: cagar budaya may leave Indonesia **only for research, cultural promotion, or exhibitions, and only with the Menteri's (minister's) permit**; Pasal 69 requires a governor/regent permit to move it across provinces ([Wikisource](https://id.wikisource.org/wiki/Undang-Undang_Republik_Indonesia_Nomor_11_Tahun_2010)). Pasal 14(2) outright bans **foreigners** from carrying cagar budaya out.
- PP No. 10/1993 likewise requires the Minister's permission to take cultural property out of Indonesia ([text](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Download/46913/PP%20No.%2010%20Th%201993.pdf)).
- The service standard is set by the Directorate of Cultural Protection (Direktorat Pelindungan Kebudayaan) — application letter, BPCB/BPK recommendation, item list with photos/age, owner legality, purpose. **Marked uncertain:** the exact 2026 submission channel and turnaround (historically ~1 month ahead, via email) come from a copy of the service standard on [Scribd](https://id.scribd.com/document/659072122/SP-a-Izin-Membawa-Benda-Cagar-Budaya-Dan-Koleksi-Museum-Ke-Luar-Wilayah-NKRI) — verify with the local BPK.

### Banned/restricted categories and enforcement
Ministry of Trade Regulation **Permendag 22/2023** (Barang yang Dilarang untuk Diekspor), as amended (20/2024, 8/2025, and **Permendag 6/2026**), prohibits exporting ([BPK record](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/260133/permendag-no-22-tahun-2023); [Permendag 6/2026](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/347246/permendag-no-6-tahun-2026)):
- `ex 9705.00.10` / `ex 9705.00.90` — collections/collectors' items of historical, archaeological, paleontological, ethnographic interest (i.e., cultural-property goods meeting the 50-year criteria);
- `ex 9706.00.00` — **antiques over 100 years old** meeting the significance criteria.
The "ex." prefix means only goods matching the criteria (not the whole HS heading) are banned. Customs enforces this under KMK 20/MK/BC/2026 ([beacukai.go.id prohibition page](https://www.beacukai.go.id/larangan-pembatasan)). Bea Cukai regularly seizes smuggled kendi, bowls, ceramics, and statues ([Bea Cukai enforcement post](https://www.facebook.com/beacukairi/posts/1306410444866812)).

### Penalties
Taking cagar budaya out of Indonesia without ministerial approval: **6 months–10 years imprisonment and a fine of Rp200,000,000–Rp1,500,000,000** (Pasal 109(1)); moving it across provinces without approval: up to 5 years and up to Rp100 million (Pasal 109(2)) ([Wikisource](https://id.wikisource.org/wiki/Undang-Undang_Republik_Indonesia_Nomor_11_Tahun_2010); [BPK law record](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/38552/uu-no-11-tahun-2010)).

### 2024–2026 changes
Ministry restructuring (Perpres 190/2024: BPCB → BPK under Kementerian Kebudayaan); Permendag 8/2025 and 6/2026 amendments to the export-ban annex; Permendikbudristek 36/2023 formalizing the ODCB/SKBCB process.

## 2. International Shipping Options from Indonesia for Antiques

### International express carriers (small parcel — most realistic for one-off family items)
- **DHL Express Indonesia.** Parcels up to **70 kg per piece** (heavier items must be palletized/crated) ([DHL Indonesia packing rules](https://www.dhl.com/id-en/home/express/shipping-and-tracking/shipping/packing-and-supplies/packing-with-care.html); [DHL large-item guide](https://www.dhl.com/discover/en-global/logistics-advice/import-export-advice/how-to-ship-large-items)). DHL handles customs clearance; duties are driven by HS code, origin, and declared value on a CIF basis ([DHL Indonesia declared-value guide](https://www.dhl.com/discover/en-id/logistics-advice/essential-guides/declared-value-customs-clearance-indonesia)). Insurance ("shipment value protection") costs **a flat IDR 170,000 or 2% of shipment value** on outbound international shipments ([DHL Indonesia insurance page](https://www.dhl.com/discover/en-id/ship-with-dhl/start-shipping/advantages-shipping-insurance)) — the most concrete insurance number found. Note: DHL does not arrange Indonesian cultural-property permits (SKBCB/export permit); that is the shipper's job (not stated explicitly on the page — **unverified, confirm with DHL**).
- **FedEx Indonesia.** First **US$100 of liability included**; higher declared value costs incrementally ([FedEx declared value page](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/shipping/declared-value.html)). FedEx's Conditions of Carriage (July 2025) treat antiques as items of extraordinary value, and antiques are among the categories where liability is limited or excluded — insurance for genuinely valuable antiques may need third-party cover ([FedEx Conditions of Carriage](https://www.fedex.com/en-lt/conditions-of-carriage/july-2025.html)). FedEx publishes [Indonesia customs clearance guidelines (PDF)](https://www.fedex.com/content/dam/fedex/apac-asia-pacific/downloads/fedex-import-clearance-guideline-en-id.pdf).
- **UPS.** Basic liability **US$100 per package**; declared value coverage up to **US$50,000 per package** (US$100,000 per pallet on Worldwide Express Freight) ([UPS value-added services pricing](https://www.ups.com/media/en/value-added_pricing_ak-hi.pdf); [UPS terms](https://www.ups.com/assets/resources/webcontent/en_US/terms_service_br.pdf)). Over 70 kg/package moves to freight products. Declared value is carrier liability, not insurance.
- **Pos Indonesia (national post, economy option).** EMS goods up to **30 kg**; "Pos Ekspor" for **0–300 kg** ([service terms](https://www.posindonesia.co.id/id/pages/syarat-dan-ketentuan-kiriman); [service overview](https://www.posindonesia.co.id/id/blogs/kenali-perbedaan-5-jenis-pengiriman-internasional-di-pos-indonesia)). Cheapest for small items but slowest; insurance/customs handling must be checked per service ([export T&C](https://www.posindonesia.co.id/id/blogs/syarat-dan-ketentuan-ekspor-barang-lewat-posind)).

### Freight forwarders / specialists (larger shipments, LCL/FCL sea freight)
- **Arche Mitra Global** (Jakarta) — freight forwarder whose marketing claims specialization in exporting art and antiques, with customs brokerage and air/sea/truck options ([site](https://archemg.co.id)). **Unverified:** the art/antique specialization claim comes from search results; the homepage itself is generic.
- **DMK Cargo** — lists a "Valuable Cargo" product and air/sea/land channels ([site](https://dmkcargo.co.id)). **Unverified:** specific high-value art/antique handling described in search results is not on the homepage.
- General established forwarders (e.g., [Keenam International](https://keenam.co.id), [Mitsui-Soko Indonesia](https://www.mitsui-soko.co.id), [Erge Asia](https://ergeasia.com)) handle LCL/FCL export plus PEB export declarations; suitability for antiques must be confirmed individually.
- Aggregators: [Easyship](https://www.easyship.com) (multi-courier labels, customs forms) and [Janio](https://www.janio.asia/resources/articles/how-to-ship-to-indonesia) focus on e-commerce cross-border; [Shipper.id](https://shipper.id/en/services/logistics-aggregator) is mainly domestic. **None found to offer Indonesian cultural-property permit support — treat as unsuitable for high-value antiques unless proven otherwise.**

### What drives cost (no reliable public rate card found — do not quote numbers)
Chargeable weight (actual vs volumetric), destination zone, speed tier, declared value (insurance ~1–2% on DHL; incremental fees on FedEx/UPS above the free US$100 liability), and accessorial fees for crating/palletizing; sea LCL is priced per cubic meter and is the economical path for furniture-scale pieces.

## Practical implications for the app

- **Track per item:** estimated age/year of manufacture, materials (flag CITES-sensitive ones like ivory, shell, rosewood, crocodile leather), dimensions/weight/crating needs, and photos — all feed the BPK assessment and the customs invoice.
- **Flag items 50+ years old** ("antique mode"): force a checklist step — obtain an SKBCB from the provincial BPK/Balai Pelestarian Kebudayaan before allowing an international listing or sale; treat items that ARE cagar budaya as export-blocked (research/exhibition permits only).
- **Store compliance artifacts:** SKBCB number/scan, export permit number, certificate of authenticity, CITES permit, ISPM-15 fumigation cert — with expiry/renewal dates, attached to the sale workflow.
- **Selling workflow checks:** destination country not on any restricted list; buyer-side import rules reminder (duties/HS 9705/9706 treatment abroad); a required honest, detailed customs invoice description (HSH documents show vague invoices are a top seizure cause).
- **Shipping step:** capture declared value (drives 1–2% insurance and liability caps: US$100 default on FedEx/UPS, US$50,000 UPS max/package) and dimensions to generate quotes; for >30–70 kg or furniture, route to a forwarder (LCL sea) rather than express parcel.
- **Never auto-generate shipping labels for flagged items** until the export-clearance checklist passes — penalties for illegal export reach 10 years prison / Rp1.5 billion (UU 11/2010 Pasal 109).
